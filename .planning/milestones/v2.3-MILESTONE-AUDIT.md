## 5. Tech Debt & Intentional Design Contracts

The audit identified no blocking technical debt. Intentional safety contracts are documented below:
- **ScriptCache Gating (Phase 12):** Chromium and Electron `ScriptCache` leaves remain `ReviewOnly` by default to safeguard extension states and offline execution until explicit recovery proof is supplied.
- **Ambiguous LaunchAgents (Phase 13):** Relative paths, PATH-based lookups, or unreadable plists are preserved as `ReviewOnly` rather than marked stale.
- **Shared Stores (Phase 10):** Docker images/volumes, OrbStack data, NuGet packages, and pnpm stores are strictly review-only in all non-manual execution modes.
- **Data Race Remediation (Phase 14):** A potential test data race in `MockCommandExecutor` under concurrent cleaner scans was resolved with `sync.RWMutex` (commit `25c8796`) and verified clean with `go test -race`.
