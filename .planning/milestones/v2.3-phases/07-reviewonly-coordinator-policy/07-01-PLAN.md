<objective>
Implement the domain model for review-only targets and enforce central Stage 1 interception within deleter.Coordinator.

Purpose: Decouple disk inventory discovery from cleanup authorization. Shared package stores, active runtime processes, and ambiguous leftovers must be visible to users for situational awareness but unconditionally barred from automatic or forced deletion.
Output: Extended domain.Target struct with semantic reason constants, Stage 1 review rejection in deleter.Coordinator with structured SkippedReviewOnly reporting, and comprehensive policy test suites.
</objective>
